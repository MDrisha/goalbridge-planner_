How was this calculated? Methodology & Formulas

This educational scenario planner uses standard time-value-of-money equations with monthly compounding and end-of-month contribution assumptions:

Basis

Monthly Rate Conversion

Annual rate / 12 = monthly rate

Annual inflation and assumed return rates are divided by 12 to derive monthly rates. Total duration is calculated as years × 12 = months.

Step 1

Future Goal Value

Today's goal cost × (1 + inflation)years

Calculates how purchasing power shifts across the target timeline. If inflation is 0%, the future goal value equals today's goal cost.

Step 2

Future Value of Current Savings

Current savings × (1 + monthly return)months

Compounding projection for current savings using monthly compounding over the total months.

Step 3

Funding Gap

Future goal value − future value of current savings

The remaining net requirement. If projected savings equal or exceed the future goal, the funding gap is ₹0.

Step 4

Monthly Contribution

Funding gap × monthly rate / ((1 + monthly rate)months − 1)

Calculates the illustrative deposit at the end of each month given an assumed positive return.

Fallback

Zero-Return Fallback

Funding gap / months

If the assumed annual return is 0%, the monthly contribution is calculated linearly without compounding as Funding gap / months, and projected growth is ₹0.

Step 5

Optional Annual Step-Up

P = Funding Gap / ∑y=0n-1 [ (1 + g)y × (((1 + r)12 − 1) / r) × (1 + r)12(n − y − 1) ]

If an annual step-up rate g is entered, contributions increase by g% each year. The starting monthly contribution P compounds in 12-month blocks. When step-up is 0%, the formula simplifies identically to the standard monthly contribution.

All formulas assume end-of-month contributions and monthly compounding intervals. Taxes, fees, and transaction costs are excluded.

HOW THIS ESTIMATE IS CALCULATED

• Future goal cost is estimated by compounding today's goal cost using the user-specified annual inflation rate over the planning horizon: P × (1 + i)years.

• Future value of current savings is calculated using monthly compounding based on the assumed annual investment return: S × (1 + r/12)12 × years.

• The funding gap is the net shortfall between the estimated future goal cost and the projected future value of current savings: max(0, Future Cost − Future Savings).

• The estimated monthly contribution is calculated using monthly compounding and end-of-month contribution annuity formulas over the remaining months. When assumed annual return is 0%, contributions are determined linearly as funding gap divided by total months.

ASSUMPTIONS

  • Inflation is an illustrative assumption entered by the user.
  • Investment return is an illustrative assumption and is not guaranteed.
  • Actual inflation, investment returns and future living costs may differ materially.
  • Current savings are assumed to remain fully allocated to this goal throughout the horizon.
  • Monthly contributions are assumed to occur at the end of each calendar month.
  • Results are mathematical illustrations and not predictions.
  • Step-up assumes the monthly contribution increases by the selected percentage once each year. This does not imply that income or affordability will increase.
