How was this calculated? Methodology & Formulas

This educational scenario planner uses standard time-value-of-money equations with monthly compounding and end-of-month contribution assumptions:

Basis

Monthly Rate Conversion

Annual rate / 12 = monthly rate

Annual inflation and assumed return rates are divided by 12 to derive monthly rates. Total duration is calculated as years × 12 = months.

Step 1

Future Goal Value

Today's goal cost × (1 + inflation)years

Calculates how purchasing power shifts across the target timeline. If inflation is 0%, the future goal value equals today's goal cost.

Step 2

Future Value of Current Savings

Current savings × (1 + monthly return)months

Compounding projection for current savings using monthly compounding over the total months.

Step 3

Funding Gap

Future goal value − future value of current savings

The remaining net requirement. If projected savings equal or exceed the future goal, the funding gap is ₹0.

Step 4

Monthly Contribution

Funding gap × monthly rate / ((1 + monthly rate)months − 1)

Calculates the illustrative deposit at the end of each month given an assumed positive return.

Fallback

Zero-Return Fallback

Funding gap / months

If the assumed annual return is 0%, the monthly contribution is calculated linearly without compounding as Funding gap / months, and projected growth is ₹0.

All formulas assume end-of-month contributions and monthly compounding intervals. Taxes, fees, and transaction costs are excluded.

Assumptions & educational disclaimer

This calculator is for educational and illustrative purposes only. It is not financial, investment, tax or legal advice and does not recommend any investment product or strategy.

Future values are estimates based on the assumptions entered. Actual inflation, investment returns and future costs may differ materially.

The assumed investment return is not guaranteed.

Calculations use annual rates divided by 12 to derive monthly rates and monthly compounding for the contribution calculation.

Monthly contributions are assumed to be made at the end of each month.

Taxes, fees, transaction costs and changes in contribution amounts are not included.
